Project overview

About this concept

ClipZ is a fictional local barbershop used to demonstrate a service-focused website. The shop, prices, schedule, and customer details on this page are sample content, not a real business listing.

The interface brings service descriptions, appointment selection, and a booking summary into one straightforward flow. The form is a front-end demonstration and does not contact a business or reserve a time.
